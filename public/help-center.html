Getting Started

This section will guide you through the basic steps of using the report generation tool.

  • Step 1: Upload your CSV data using the "Load Data" button.
  • Step 2: Choose an account type (AWS or RDS/APP) in the dropdown menu.
  • Step 3: Use the buttons to switch between Original Data, Parsed Data, and Compared Data.

Key Features

  • Original Data: View your raw uploaded data without modification.
  • Parsed Data: View the data after it’s been processed and organized.
  • Compared Data: Compare two different datasets to spot differences.

Frequently Asked Questions

To upload a CSV file, click the "Load Data" button and select your CSV file from your computer.

Ensure your file is in CSV format and properly structured. If it continues to not display, try refreshing the page.

Troubleshooting

  • Problem: Data is not loading after clicking the "Load Data" button.
  • Solution: Ensure your CSV file is correctly formatted and contains no errors.
  • Problem: The tables are not showing properly.
  • Solution: Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache.
